About this project

About HerbID Iloilo

An AI-powered identification and geospatial information system for medicinal herbs in Iloilo Province

General objective

This study covers medicinal herbs found in the Province of Iloilo. It uses AI technology to identify plants from pictures and shows the herb's name, its uses, and location. The system is designed for students, herbal practitioners, and local community members who want to learn about medicinal herbs.

Specific objectives

  • Create a database of 18 medicinal herbs in Iloilo
  • Train an AI model to recognize herbs from images
  • Design a mobile-friendly capture/upload interface
  • Display local, scientific names and medicinal uses
  • Show herb locations in Lemery, Iloilo on a map
  • Include a smart search feature for herb information

Scope

This study covers medicinal herbs in the Province of Iloilo, specifically within the municipality of Lemery. It uses AI technology for plant identification from images. The system shows herb names, uses, and mapped locations.

Delimitations

  • Only herbs already in the database can be identified
  • Results depend on image clarity and model training quality
  • For identification and information only — not a substitute for medical advice
  • Focuses solely on Iloilo Province herbs
